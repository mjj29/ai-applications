## How to read each chart

- **delta (above)** — two shots tinted cyan/red; fringes = differences. Comes as real-frame
  (`.delta.mp4`) and **wireframe** (`.delta_wire.mp4`) versions. Time axis is event-aligned:
  the draw and follow-through are **DTW-aligned by actual motion** (so a pause or faster pull
  lines up, not just the endpoints), the hold is the shorter shot's in full (longer shot's
  static middle cut), and release is anchored. Played at half speed. Tune in config `timeline`:
  `slow_factor`, `draw_s`/`follow_s`, `max_hold_s`, or `mode: release` for plain real time.
- **overlay.mp4 (wireframe video)** — all shots' skeletons on that same aligned timeline
  (colour per shot, white = mean); tight where consistent, spreading where they differ.
- **`…segment.png`** — how shots were found: green bands = your holds/shots, red lines =
  releases. Check there's one band per arrow (wind let-downs are correctly left unshaded).
- **`…overlay_release.png`** — every shot's stick figure at release; thick black = average.
  Lines lying on top = consistent; a stray line = that shot differed.
- **`…angles.png`** — joint angles vs time (0 = release). Narrow grey band = consistent;
  wide = inconsistent. Tight during the aim (left of 0) is what you want.
- **`…tracks.png`** — the paths your hands trace; overlapping = repeatable.
- **`…ghost.mp4` / `…ghost_release.png`** — your real footage from every shot blended,
  body-aligned. Sharp where identical, blurred where shots differ. The background blurs
  because the body is pinned, not the scene.

*Units: time is seconds from release; distances are "body-lengths" (one torso), so camera
distance and your size are removed and only form remains.*
